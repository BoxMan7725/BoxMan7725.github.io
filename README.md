# The Orange Box
###### The Official Lore of Boxman

## Exposition

### The Beginning

  It all started at the beginning of the universe, when Boxman created a magical fruit. This fruit was the orange. Boxman loves green(will come in handy as good info later) Boxman loved oranges. Boxman loved oranges so much he made more. Then he made even more. Then, can you guess what he made next? More oranges. After about 19 oranges Boxman decided to make the entire innards of the universe. After exploring the universe in his orange powered *Boxmobile* he discovered Earth. He loved humans, and they impressed Boxman so much that he introduced them to oranges. Unfortunately for them, they didn't really like oranges. In fact, the humans hated oranges. This angered Boxman so much that he destroyed the world. After **Boxman Destroyed The World** he rebuilt it with better humans. Humans that liked oranges. This world was better. to Boxman's dismay, not all the people liked oranges. Most of them did, so he was ok with it for now. Boxman got lonely watching humans do random ass shit, so he decided to put his all mighty hand into the ocean and grab the first thing he saw. That thing was a shark. This shark was a disappointment in Boxmans eyes. This shark had committed a horrible sin, for this shark did not particularly enjoy oranges. Boxman looked upon this shark with shame and fixed it. It made this shark **LOVE** oranges, and gave it some arms and legs. Boxman then named his new pet with a beautiful name, **Shark With Arms and Legs**. 


### The Bunderworld

  Around the time the universe was created, a second dimension formed called the Bunderworld. The Bunderworld is named after the mix of underworld, and boxes. The king of the Bunderworld is Boxoclese. Boxoclese created beets because he is an insane fucking horrible person and likes them for some fucking reason. He loves the color red, and hates Boxman. Boxoclese also hates oranges. Boxoclese created hitler to destroy orange juice, but his accent led to a misunderstanding that created the holocaust. Then he created Napoleon to destroy all oranges, but he failed. This led Boxoclese to banish Napoleon to a island filled with horrible things. Napoleon got a second chance and failed, and then was banished once more. Boxoclese created the idea of wearing sandals and socks at the same time, and he created axe body spray to brainwash people into hating oranges and Boxman.


#### The Other Boxgods:

The other Boxgods were all created by Boxman to help control the universe.

**Boximus Prime:** Boximus Prime is the boxgod of sameday delivery and creativity. He is known to take many forms like animals and buildings. Boximus Prime moves with incredible speed and can get anywhere in the same day as started traveling no matter how long the distance. Boximus Prime is always in between forms and doesn't use one as his main form usually.

**Banos:** Banos is the boxgod of order. He keeps everything in balance, as all things should be. Banos takes the apearance of a large purple man.

**Muhammad Ali:** Muhammed Ali is a boxgod and always has been. He is the boxgod of fighting. Ever wonder why he **BOXES**?????? Muhammad Ali takes the apearance of a human boxer.

**Bandalf:** Bandalf is the boxgod of magic and adventure, he likes to pick on tiny short people and make them brawl with fantastical creatures like dragons. Bandalf takes the apearance of a tall wizard in a grey robe.

**Balter White:** Balter White is the boxgod of drugs, and getting high. He is the one who knocks. Balter White takes on the apearance of a bald white man with cancer and a menacing moustache.

**Toboxius:** Toboxius is the boxgod of technology and automation. He likes to lock people out of his wonderful home. Toboxius is a half Boxperson, half machine.

**Freddy Boxbear:** Freddy Boxbear is the boxgod of fear. Freddy Boxbear takes the apearance of a animatronic endoskeleton wearing a box costume. It is said that Toboxius created him, but Freddy Boxbear gained sentience of his own after reading the issue of iron man in which ultron was introduced.


### Damoclese and The Forgotten Eight Boxgod(Myths & Legends):

Long ago in what is now modern day mexico, a horrible eight god was created. This god was named Bendrix. Bendrix was once the god of purity, but was corrupted by Boxoclese. Bendrix was then turned into the Boxgod of chaos and gluttony.

Our story starts in the country of Boxia during 800 BGC(Before Box Christ). In Boxia lived a man named Damoclese. Damoclese was a genius music maker who couldn't stop talking about his newest work. He called it 'The Unbeatable OST'. Damoclese was a fair and morally good person by heart. He was trained by the best mentor in Boxia. Long ago he was told to rule the world by his mentor. Damoclese took those words to heart. Unfortunatley many crops and livestock had gone missing due to the infection of Bendrix. Bendrix had been going around and eating everything causign famines all accross the country of Boxia. Damoclese knew he had to stop Bendrix. If he could prove himself to the gods, and gain enough power to rule over the lands. He alone wasn't powerful enough but with the help of some magical creatures and beast, he knew he could do it. Damoclese was about to go when he was stopped by his best friend Tyson. Tyson and Damoclese had a bound stronger than steel, and Tyson was scared for him. Their friendship was unbreakable, one might even say **unbeatable**. "Perhaps," Max suggeested, "You should listen to the Unbeatalbe OST. It will put your mind at ease my dear friend," Damoclese gave Tyson one last hug(the most affection Damoclese ever showed anyone after the death of his parents sent him away for his baldness) before leaving. "Stay safe out there," Tyson yelled after Damoclese, but alas it was too late. For Damoclese had already ran off in search for the mythical Blubber worm. The Blubber worm is a fatty large slightly transparent worm. It has a mischevious grin and is about the size of a modern day car. Don't let its goofy apearance deter you, the Blubber worm is a creature of imense power. *Perhaps the Blubber worm is powerful enough to take down a god like Bendrix,* Damoclese thought to himself. Damoclese wasn't even sure if he could defeat the likes of Bendrix, but he knew he had to try or die trying. After ten long weeks of travelling Damoclese finally made it to the temple of Blubber in search for the mythical beast. In order to tame the strong beast you must best it in a game of fencing. Before even getting an attempt you must first give an offering of an Oxocaan Swallowtail(an extremley rare butterfly). Damoclese had spent a unholy amount of time finding and catching the butterfly. Damoclese knelt before the Blubber worm and offered the Oxocaan Swallowtail. The Blubber worms grin widened as it ate the Butterfly then let out a roar that shook the earth. Wings sprouted from the Blubber worm and arms formed from its sides. Instead of hands it had hardened blades. It had six blades and a smile that could melt your heart ;). It let out a roar then dashed at Damoclese. Damoclese rolled out of the way and the Blubber worm shot a fang from its blubbery thorax. The fang was quickly deflected by his rapier, as he pulled out his revolved and sent a bullet straight for its thorax. The goal was not to kill the blubber worm(if he even could), only to best it in battle. This would earn its respect. The bullet was deflected by one of the Blubber worms blades. The Blubber worm flew towards Damoclese at an insane speed. All of its blades concentrated to stab Damoclese through the chest. Damoclese paried with his rapier then dashed back and shot at the thorax, this time hitting. the back of the Blubber worms thorax. Then Damoclese let out a yell as he slashed it's thorax with his rapier. The beast flew back and got ready to let out an extremley powerful attack coined the six blad ballet. An extremley fast six blade attack that has left the Blubber Worms strongest oponents in peices. The Blubber worm flew up a hundred feet and began a free fall at incredible speed. It landed on Damoclese with a collision that split the ground. Damoclese delfected the first attack which nearly broke his sword. The next five attacks came as blurs. Luckily, Damoclese using his incredible fencing experience, deflected. Then Damoclese with all of his might and rage put into one fist, punched the blubber worm square in the face. A crack that could be heard all the way back in Boxia, was let out and the Blubber worm flew back. Damoclese knew he had finally won. The Blubber worms blade arms retracted, and so did its wings. Its thorax fattened and it returned back to its normal state. The Blubber worm smiled at Damoclese and sat eagrly. Damoclese spoke, "Blubber worm, I am here to ask of your asistance in the killing of Bendrix," the Blubber worms grin slightly faded, "He has been terrorizing everyone and has turned to the side of Boxoclese," The Blubber worm looked blankly at Damoclese for awhile and thoguht to itself.
